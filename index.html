Hydra
Art Walk 2026

Opens
21 June 2026

Hydra Art Walk is a hybrid group exhibition spread across the entire island — physical posters by participating artists placed in the harbour, alleyways, and on hillside walls, each one activatable as a web-based AR experience. No app needed. Point your phone at a poster and the artwork comes alive: painting, sculpture, poetry, music — extended into augmented reality, directly in your browser.

Each poster also carries a personal audio by the artist — what brought them to Hydra, and why we all love this island. 🎧

Across the island, physical posters invite you on a Hydra Art Walk — each one a portal into painting, sculpture, poetry and music, extended into AR. Plus a series of studio visits & events throughout the season.
