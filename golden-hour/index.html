Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need experience with meditation? +
No. This experience is designed for everyone — whether you've meditated your whole life or you're completely new. There's no right or wrong way to be present.
Can I bring a friend? +
Absolutely. We have limited spots (10 total) to keep the experience intimate. Book early so you can both join us.
What if the weather is bad? +
We have a covered area and can adapt the location if needed. The experience happens rain or shine — we work with nature.
What's the cancellation policy? +
100% refund (minus 10€ administrative fee) if cancelled until 48 hours before the event. After that, you can credit your payment toward a future date. Life happens — we get it.
Will I be transformed in 60 minutes? +
This is not about dramatic transformation. It's about coming home to yourself for one hour. What happens after is up to you — but many women find clarity, permission, and peace.
What if I feel emotional during the experience? +
Stay in the moment. Everything is allowed. And this will be a joyful group — no worries. We're here to feel, breathe, and laugh together. Whatever comes up, it's all part of the magic.
Questions or special needs? +
Reach out via Instagram @silendmindfulness. If you have anxiety about groups, accessibility needs, or anything else — DM me so I can make sure you feel safe and supported.
